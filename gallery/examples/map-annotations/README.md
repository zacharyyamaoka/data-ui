# Published figures, called out on the map

Seven published 2024 figures for Metro Vancouver's waste facilities, each pinned to its facility with a leader line and a label: what was received, disposed or burned, and the cap it is measured against. Click a label for its source and page.

- **App:** Map
- **Replaces:** an annotated slide made in Keynote over a map screenshot.
- **Shows:** `{type: labels, text, detail}`: HTML callouts on MapLibre markers, fanned out so nearby ones don't stack; `notes:` for free annotations at a point.
- **Data:** `facts.json`: the City of Vancouver's 2024 Vancouver Landfill annual report (vancouver.ca, read via the Internet Archive's copy of the same URL) and Metro Vancouver's solid waste system fact sheet (metrovancouver.org); each figure checked against its cited page and restated with attribution (Source: City of Vancouver; Metro Vancouver). Facility points © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
